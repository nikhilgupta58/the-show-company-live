Every fest committee faces the same three problems: artists who don't reply, quotes that change after the first call, and production vendors who've never done a campus show. The Show Company fixes all three. We've produced 500+ shows including campus events across India, and we work with student committees the way a professional promoter would — written quotes, signed contracts, and a production team that has actually built stages on college grounds. For college shows, we have worked with institutions like Graphic Era, DPS, and many more.

Tell us your fest dates, expected footfall and budget, and we'll propose a line-up that fits: a headline comedian for your comedy night, an EDM or DJ night with club-grade sound and lighting, singers and live bands for pro-nights, and anchors to run the show. Our partners include OML and Oriole Entertainment on the artist side and VIGOR MENTORS for sound and lighting, so quality doesn't depend on whichever local vendor is free that weekend.

College shows are best locked 1–3 months ahead — fest season calendars (February–April and September–November) fill up fast, and headline artists price higher the closer you get to the date.

Questions

College Fest FAQs

What budget do we need for a fest headliner?+

Emerging comedians and DJs start around ₹50,000–₹1.5 lakh; well-known touring names typically run ₹2–8 lakh plus production and travel. Share your real budget and we'll show you the strongest line-up it buys — see the pricing guide for detail.

Can you work with our college's payment process?+

Yes — we regularly work with institutional advance/settlement cycles, purchase orders and GST invoicing. Tell us your process during the enquiry and we'll structure the contract to match.

Do you bring sound and lighting or use ours?+

Either. Our partner VIGOR MENTORS can deliver the full rig, or our production team can audit and run your existing campus setup against the artist's technical rider.

When should we start planning?+

1–3 months before your fest. Earlier is cheaper: artist calendars for fest season fill months ahead, and confirmed dates unlock better rates.
