The hour that matters

The room comes alive or checks its phones.

The hardest part of a corporate event isn't the venue or the catering — it's the hour where the room either comes alive or checks its phones. The Show Company builds that hour. We are a live entertainment production company with 500+ shows delivered across India, working with HR teams, admin teams and event agencies to put the right entertainment in front of the right audience.

One brief gets you a curated menu of options — a corporate-safe stand-up comedian, a live band, a DJ-led evening, an emcee to hold the run-of-show together, or a combination. We then execute end-to-end: artist contracts, travel, technical production with our partner VIGOR MENTORS, stage and backdrop design, and a producer on the ground on event day. You get one point of contact and one invoice.

Booking lead time for corporate events is typically 2–4 weeks depending on complexity; peak season dates (December, financial-year-end) are best locked 6–8 weeks out.

Questions

Corporate Booking FAQs

What does corporate entertainment cost in India?+

Most mid-size corporate events land between ₹2–8 lakh for a headline act plus production; intimate offsites can start under ₹1 lakh. Our pricing guide breaks down what moves the number.

Can the content be kept corporate-appropriate?+

Yes. We brief every artist on your audience, industry and red lines before the show, and we only shortlist performers with a track record in corporate rooms.

Do you work outside the metros?+

Yes — we've produced events in 50+ cities including Dehradun, Jaipur, Lucknow and Indore. Artist travel is built into the quote so there are no surprise costs.

How far in advance should we book?+

2–4 weeks for most events; 6–8 weeks for December and year-end dates, when artist calendars fill fastest.
