How Much Does It Cost to Book a Comedian in India?

Honest budget bands from a team that has booked 100+ artists and produced 500+ shows — no "price on request" games.

The short answer: booking a stand-up comedian in India typically costs ₹50,000–₹1.5 lakh for emerging club-circuit comedians, ₹2–8 lakh for established touring names, and ₹10 lakh and above for national headliners with large social followings or OTT specials. Add production (sound, lighting, stage) and artist travel on top — typically ₹40,000–₹3 lakh depending on venue size and city.

Fee Bands by Artist Tier

Artist Tier Typical Fee Best For
Emerging / club circuit ₹50K – ₹1.5L Club nights, small college shows, intimate offsites
Established touring comedian ₹2L – ₹8L Fest headline nights, corporate annual days, ticketed shows
National headliner (OTT special / mass following) ₹10L+ Marquee corporate galas, large auditorium and arena shows

Ranges are indicative for 2026 and vary by date, city and format. A specific artist's quote can sit outside these bands — always confirm against real availability.

What Moves the Price

  • Date and day of week. Saturday nights in fest or wedding season can price 2–3x a weekday. December and February–March are the most contested calendars.
  • City and travel. Shows in metros where the artist lives cost less than fly-in dates; non-metro venues add flights, stay and ground transport for artist and crew.
  • Audience size and format. A 45-minute corporate set, a 90-minute ticketed solo, and a fest slot are priced differently by the same artist — the brief matters as much as the name.
  • Production scope. A club has a mic and a spot; an auditorium needs PA, monitors, lighting, backdrop and a technical crew. Budget ₹40K–₹3L depending on venue scale.
  • Who's booking. Direct-to-manager quotes often inflate for corporate buyers. Booking through a production company with ongoing artist relationships usually lands closer to real market rates — and comes with a contract.

Three Realistic Budget Examples

College comedy night · ~₹2–3 lakh all-in

A well-known touring comedian for the fest's comedy night, campus auditorium production, travel included. The most-booked package on the college fest circuit.

Corporate annual day · ~₹4–8 lakh all-in

An established name with corporate-safe material, 45–60 minute set, full sound and lighting at a hotel ballroom, emcee included. Typical for 300–800 person corporate events.

Ticketed auditorium show · ₹8 lakh+

A headliner in a 800–2,000 seat venue with full production, ticketing and promotion — priced as a revenue-sharing or flat-fee show production deal.
