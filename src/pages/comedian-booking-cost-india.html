How Much Does It Cost to Book a Comedian in India?

Honest budget bands from a team that has booked 100+ artists and produced 500+ shows — no "price on request" games.

At a glance

The short answer

The short answer: booking a stand-up comedian in India typically costs ₹50,000–₹1.5 lakh for emerging club-circuit comedians, ₹2–8 lakh for established touring names, and ₹10 lakh and above for national headliners with large social followings or OTT specials. Add production (sound, lighting, stage) and artist travel on top — typically ₹40,000–₹3 lakh depending on venue size and city.

Pricing factors

What Moves the Price

  1. Date and day of week. Saturday nights in fest or wedding season can price 2–3x a weekday. December and February–March are the most contested calendars.

  2. City and travel. Shows in metros where the artist lives cost less than fly-in dates; non-metro venues add flights, stay and ground transport for artist and crew.

  3. Audience size and format. A 45-minute corporate set, a 90-minute ticketed solo, and a fest slot are priced differently by the same artist — the brief matters as much as the name.

  4. Production scope. A club has a mic and a spot; an auditorium needs PA, monitors, lighting, backdrop and a technical crew. Budget ₹40K–₹3L depending on venue scale.

  5. Who's booking. Direct-to-manager quotes often inflate for corporate buyers. Booking through a production company with ongoing artist relationships usually lands closer to real market rates — and comes with a contract.

For companies

Corporate Event Pricing

Budgeting specifically for a corporate event? The same fee bands above map cleanly onto company events: intimate corporate offsites can start under ₹1 lakh with an emerging comedian, mid-size corporate events with a headline act plus production typically run ₹2–8 lakh — our most common corporate annual day booking lands around ₹4–8 lakh all-in for a 300–800 person audience — and large corporate galas with a national headliner can exceed ₹10 lakh.

These numbers cover the artist and production — if you also need someone to run the whole event, from booking to on-ground show management, that's full-service event management, not just artist booking.
