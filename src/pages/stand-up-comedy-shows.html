Stand-Up Booking FAQs

How much does it cost to book a stand-up comedian in India?

Emerging comedians typically start around ₹50,000–₹1 lakh; established touring names range ₹2–8 lakh; national headliners go well beyond. The venue, city, date and format all move the number — see our comedian booking cost guide for detailed ranges.

Can you suggest comedians that fit our audience and budget?

Yes — that's the core of what we do. Share your city, audience profile and budget band and we'll send a curated shortlist with real availability, instead of a generic roster page.

Do you handle sound, lighting and the stage too?

Yes. Through our sound and lighting partner VIGOR MENTORS we deliver full technical production — PA, monitors, stage wash, backdrop and show cues — alongside the artist booking.

Which cities do you cover?

We operate pan-India — Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata, Jaipur, Dehradun and 50+ cities so far. If your city has a venue, we can produce a show in it.
