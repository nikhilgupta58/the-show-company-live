Who handles everything?

Search "event management company" or "event organiser" in India and you'll mostly find directory listings and rankings — not a straight answer to the question that actually matters: who handles everything, so you don't have to manage a comedian, a sound vendor and a stage contractor as three separate relationships. That's the gap The Show Company fills. We are a live entertainment production company with 500+ shows and 100+ artists booked across 50+ cities in India — built to run the whole event, not just supply the talent.

An artist booking agency's job ends when a contract is signed. Ours doesn't. We handle artist negotiation and contracts, travel logistics, stage and backdrop design, sound and lighting through our production partner VIGOR MENTORS, venue coordination, and a producer on the ground on event day — with one point of contact and one invoice, whether you're planning a corporate gala, a college fest, a comedy night or a large auditorium show.

Questions

Event Management FAQs

What does a full-service event management company actually do?+

End-to-end production: artist booking and management, stage design and setup, sound and lighting (through our partner VIGOR MENTORS), venue sourcing and coordination, technical crew, on-ground show management, and post-event wrap-up — handled from concept to curtain call so you don't have to juggle vendors.

How is an event management company different from an artist booking agency?+

A booking agency finds you an artist and stops there. A full-service event management company also handles contracts, travel, technical production, stage and backdrop design, and a producer on the ground on event day — one point of contact and one invoice instead of separate vendors for talent, sound and staging.

What does event management cost in India?+

Intimate offsites and college comedy nights can start under ₹1 lakh; most mid-size corporate events and campus fests with a headline act plus production land between ₹2–8 lakh; large galas and auditorium shows with a national headliner can exceed ₹10 lakh. Our pricing guide breaks down what moves the number.

Which cities in India do you operate in?+

We're pan-India, having produced shows in 50+ cities including Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata, Dehradun, Jaipur, Lucknow and Indore. Artist and crew travel is built into the quote, so there are no surprise costs outside metros.
