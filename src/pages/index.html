Who We Are

Great laughter brings people together.

Expanding into Advertising Brand Collaborations

The Show Company is a live entertainment production company based in India, founded to curate and deliver premium stand-up comedy shows, college fests, corporate events, and large-scale auditorium productions across the country. Operating in 50+ cities pan-India with 500+ shows produced and 100+ artists booked, The Show Company handles every detail from artist booking and stage design to sound, lighting, and post-event support.

Beyond comedy, we design vibrant live experiences at colleges, universities, and cultural venues — from energetic music gigs and EDM nights to engaging campus festivals and large-scale auditorium productions. Our team manages every detail, from artist booking and stage design to sound, lighting, and post-event support, working closely with our partners to ensure a flawless experience every time.

Our mission is simple: to create high-quality, memorable entertainment that connects audiences, celebrates creativity, and leaves every crowd smiling long after the curtains close. We are also expanding into advertising and brand collaborations, helping companies connect with audiences through the power of live entertainment.

What sets us apart is how we work: one point of contact from the first enquiry to the final bow. We shortlist artists against your budget and audience, handle contracts and travel, coordinate the venue and technical crew, and put a producer on the ground on show day itself — so colleges, corporates, and event organisers don't have to juggle separate vendors for talent, sound, and staging.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of events does The Show Company organize?+

The Show Company specializes in college shows, corporate events, and large-scale auditorium performances. Our services include standup comedy shows, EDM DJ nights, singing concerts, and live band performances. Whether it's a campus fest, corporate gala, or public auditorium event, we handle end-to-end production including artist booking, stage design, sound and lighting (through our partner VIGOR MENTORS), venue coordination, and post-event support.

How do I book a show for my college or company?+

You can reach us through the enquiry form on this website, email us at contact@theshowcompany.in, or call us directly. For college shows, we recommend booking 2–3 months in advance. For corporate events, we can accommodate shorter timelines depending on availability. Our team will work with you to understand your requirements and create a customized proposal that fits your budget and vision.

I'm an artist. How can I collaborate with The Show Company?+

We're always looking for talented artists. Send your portfolio, showreel, and a brief introduction to contact@theshowcompany.in. Our talent acquisition team reviews all submissions and reaches out to artists whose style aligns with our upcoming projects. We offer management, booking, and full production support to help artists focus on their craft while we handle the logistics.

What cities do you operate in?+

The Show Company is based in India and operates pan-India across 50+ cities. We have executed shows in Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Dehradun, Jaipur, and numerous other cities. For college shows, we have worked with institutions like Graphic Era, DPS, and many more. We can also arrange international events on request.

What is included in your event production services?+

Our end-to-end production includes artist booking and management, stage design and setup, world-class sound and lighting (through our partner VIGOR MENTORS), venue sourcing and coordination, technical crew, event management on the day, promotion support, and post-event wrap-up. We handle everything from concept to curtain call so you can focus entirely on enjoying the show.

How far in advance should I book?+

For major events with headlining artists, we recommend 3–6 months in advance to secure the best talent and venues. College fests typically require 1–3 months of lead time, and corporate events can sometimes be accommodated in as little as 2–4 weeks depending on complexity. The earlier you reach out, the better the artist selection and venue availability.
